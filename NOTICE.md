The DnD5e 2024 System in `src/systems/dnd5e-2024` adapts SRD 5.2.1 class, subclass, species, background, feat, spell and equipment rules into project-specific Feature data, and stores source passages for display. Extraction normalizes PDF layout while preserving the source wording; mechanical definitions and implementation notes are project additions. The source document is [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), SHA-256 `8974902d109d6e63672d7c490bde9ccf052410503d9cfa768237154fbc5e3d87`. The twelve DnD5e 2024 sample characters and the multiclass sample are original builds from SRD options; their names and descriptions are this project's own. No D&D Beyond or other non-SRD content is included.
