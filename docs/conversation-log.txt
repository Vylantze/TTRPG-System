I’ve reviewed PF2e’s core framework and will treat **Pathfinder2e**, **Pathfinder 2e**, and **PF2e** as interchangeable in our discussions.

My working understanding covers:

- **Resolution:** d20 checks against a DC, with critical success, success, failure, and critical failure. Results 10 above or below the DC change the outcome; natural 20s and 1s adjust its degree.
- **Proficiency:** trained, expert, master, and legendary proficiency normally include character level, making level differences mechanically significant.
- **Action economy:** three actions per turn plus a reaction. Repeated attacks usually incur −5 and then −10 penalties, giving movement, defense, and support meaningful competing value.
- **Character building:** ancestry, heritage, background, class, attributes, skills, and several categories of feats.
- **Play structure:** encounters, exploration, and downtime use different time scales and procedures. These foundations are documented in [Player Core’s rules](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2263) and [character creation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2027).
- **GM framework:** encounter budgets depend on enemy levels relative to the party, with adjustments for party size. [Encounter-building rules](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=994).

For future analysis, I’ll use **Remastered PF2e as the default**, identify relevant legacy differences, and check specific rules as needed. Paizo describes the Remaster as a revision of the same edition, with largely compatible earlier material. [Paizo’s Remaster FAQ](https://paizo.com/pathfinder/remaster/faq).
